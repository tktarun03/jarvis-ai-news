EU court adviser questions Gemini's reuse of news articles

An EU court adviser said Gemini may infringe publishers' rights when its answers recognisably reuse their expression. The opinion is non-binding, with a judgment still pending; Google highlighted the adviser's support for the text-and-data-mining exception.
