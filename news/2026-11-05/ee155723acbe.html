India warns financial firms about AI-assisted cyberattacks

India's I4C warned banks and other financial firms that AI agents are accelerating financially motivated attacks. Its advisory recommends payment limits, beneficiary-name checks and monitoring for unusual transaction speeds alongside existing CERT-In safeguards.
