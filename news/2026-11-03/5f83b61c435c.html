Anthropic open-sources FlashProve to prove which model weights produced an answer

Anthropic released FlashProve, an open-source research prototype that uses cryptographic proofs to show a model's response came from committed weights without revealing those weights. It is licensed under Apache 2.0 and labelled an unmaintained research artifact.
