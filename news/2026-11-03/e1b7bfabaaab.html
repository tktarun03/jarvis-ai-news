New York Times and three US papers sue Brave over AI use of their journalism

The New York Times Company and the publishers of the New York Daily News, Chicago Tribune and Orlando Sentinel filed separate federal complaints against Brave Software on 2 November. They allege Brave copied and distributed their journalism through its search index, AI services and browser without permission.
