IndiaMART puts its data behind logins to hold off Google AI search and AI agents

IndiaMART has moved content behind logins, blocked unrestricted scraping and restricted its data from Google Search and AI agents, MediaNama reported from the company's Q2 FY27 analyst call. CEO Dinesh Agarwal said the company is willing to give up 10-20% of unique business enquiries to protect its data.
