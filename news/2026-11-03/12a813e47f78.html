OpenAI pledges its compute campuses will cut power use when grids are strained

OpenAI launched a Grid Protectors Program under which its future compute campuses will reduce electricity use when grids are under strain, starting in Georgia. The first site, a planned campus in Effingham County, has an agreement for up to 1,000 megawatts of flexible demand response.
