NVIDIA reports growing adoption of its open-source wireless AI tools

NVIDIA says its open-source 5G and 6G tools have passed one million downloads and are used by more than 8,000 research and academic institutions. Its update describes projects combining wireless simulation, AI training and live network experiments, including research into more responsive connected robots.
