IIT Delhi and Walmart Global Tech agree ₹9 crore AI research partnership

IIT Delhi and Walmart Global Tech have announced a four-year, ₹9 crore initiative at the university's Yardi School of Artificial Intelligence. It will support AI, machine learning and data science research through fellowships, faculty training and improved research infrastructure.
