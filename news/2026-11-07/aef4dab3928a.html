OpenAI closes $11 billion in funding, Reuters source says

Reuters reports that OpenAI closed $11 billion in initial funding at a $1.25 trillion valuation, citing a person familiar with the deal. Investors told Reuters that SoftBank and MGX participated; the amount raised remains below the original target of up to $30 billion.
