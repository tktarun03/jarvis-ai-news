Marvell announces $115 billion Lumentum deal for AI connectivity

Marvell announced an all-stock agreement to acquire Lumentum for $115 billion including debt, expanding its optical components business for AI data centres. The transaction is expected to close in the first half of 2028; it is not a completed acquisition.
