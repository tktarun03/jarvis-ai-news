NeuroDX reports early Sphota model results awaiting independent evaluation

Indian startup NeuroDX says its 240-million-parameter Sphota language model achieved comparable benchmark performance to a Liquid AI model with less training data. AIM's interview makes clear that these are preliminary company results, without independent public evaluation, and that the gains may change as the model scales.
