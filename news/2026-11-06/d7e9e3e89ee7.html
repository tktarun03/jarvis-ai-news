Google launches Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash with 15-second video generation

Google made Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash generally available through the Gemini API and Gemini for business, adding native video creation up to 15 seconds and conversational editing. Google says 720p generation now costs $0.07 per second, a 30% reduction from Omni 1.1.
