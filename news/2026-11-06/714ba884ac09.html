METR estimates faster Anthropic model progress, with substantial uncertainty

METR estimates Anthropic's benchmark capabilities have improved about 1.9 times faster since October 2025 as AI contributes to development. The assessment carries high uncertainty and says AI cannot yet make major, fully autonomous contributions to the most important training stages.
