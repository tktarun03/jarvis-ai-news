Quess reports rising share of AI, data and security hiring in India's GCCs

A Quess report puts AI, data and cybersecurity at 30.3% of Indian global capability centres' technology hiring demand in July–September, up from 25.8% in the preceding quarter. It reports approximately 14% growth in demand for AI, data and analytics professionals, even as overall GCC tech hiring demand fell 3.5%.
